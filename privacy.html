Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Generational Outreach ("we", "us"), owned by Michael Lozada-Longog in Waianae, Hawaiʻi, respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you contact us or fill out our form, we collect what you give us: your name, business name, email, phone number, message, and whether you agreed to receive text messages.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to your inquiry, schedule and remind you about appointments, provide our services, and send text messages if you agreed to them.

SMS / mobile information

We do not share, sell, rent or trade mobile information or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. The only exception is service providers that help us deliver messages, such as our SMS carrier or platform, and they may use the information only to provide that service.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

To stop text messages, reply STOP at any time. To see, correct or delete your information, email michaellozadalongog@gmail.com or call (808) 364-9303.

Security & retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, and we keep it only as long as we need it for the purposes above.
