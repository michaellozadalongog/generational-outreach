Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Generational Outreach ("we", "us"), owned by Michael Lozada-Longog in Waianae, Hawaiʻi, respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you contact us or fill out our form, we collect what you give us: your name, business name, email, phone number, message, and whether you agreed to receive text messages.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to your inquiry, schedule and remind you about appointments, provide our services, and, only if you checked the optional SMS box, send informational texts about your request and appointments. We do not send marketing texts.

SMS / mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, or when the law requires it. This excludes mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data and consent, which are never shared with third parties.

Your choices

To stop text messages, reply STOP at any time. To see, correct or delete your information, email michaellozadalongog@gmail.com or call (808) 364-9303.

Security & retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, and we keep it only as long as we need it for the purposes above.
